# Illustration sources

These illustrations depict Chino Kafuu and Tippy from *Is the Order a Rabbit?*.
They are separate from the HTML5 UP template and its license. Artwork remains
the property of its respective creators; source links are also available in
the homepage footer.

- `chino-coffee.webp`: coffee and blue-petal illustration, collected at
  https://www.duitang.com/blog/?id=661555343 . The collection page does not
  identify the original artist, so no artist attribution is inferred.
- `chino-lace.webp`: blue gingham and lace illustration, collected at
  https://wall.alphacoders.com/big.php?i=680890 . The listed uploader is not
  presented as the artist.
- `chino-cafe.webp`: cafe illustration by あるか, credited by
  https://www.zerochan.net/2722801 .

Files were resized proportionally and encoded as WebP for local delivery.
The existing `landscape.webp` is the previous generated landscape asset and
is no longer used by the homepage.
